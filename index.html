Why we’re doing this

Supporting the V Foundation.

We started PickleWithPurpose because cancer has personally affected both of our families, and we have lost loved ones to the disease. We wanted to turn something fun and community-focused into a way to support a cause that means a lot to us.

By bringing people together through pickleball, our goal is to raise awareness and funds in support of the V Foundation for Cancer Research while creating something positive from experiences that have impacted our families.

All proceeds from PickleWithPurpose ticket sales will be donated to the V Foundation for Cancer Research.

Meet the founders

About Gaia & Rafi

Hi! We’re Gaia and Rafi, two high school students who created PickleWithPurpose to bring people together while giving back to a cause that has personal meaning to both of our families.

We wanted to build something that combines community, competition, and purpose in a way that feels social and positive. Through pickleball events and fundraisers, we hope to raise awareness, support cancer research, and encourage other young people to get involved in making a difference.
